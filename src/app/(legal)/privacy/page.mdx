## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you create an account with us, we may collect:

- **Profile Data**: Any additional profile information you choose to provide

### Usage Information
We automatically collect certain information when you use our Service:
- **Loadout Data**: Custom loadouts you create, including operator selections, weapon configurations, and attachment preferences
- **Analytics Data**: General usage statistics, page views, and interaction patterns (collected via Vercel Analytics)
- **Technical Information**: IP address, browser type, device information, and access times

### Game-Related Data
- **Rainbow Six Siege Game Data**: Information about operators, weapons, gadgets, and attachments (this is public game data from Ubisoft's Rainbow Six Siege)
